2. How competitions work

2.1 Game IDs

You must save your real in-game name (and ID if asked) for each game you play. Results are verified against these names. Your name for a game is locked after your first match; contact support to fix a genuine mistake.

2.2 1v1 challenges and bounty matches

  • Friendly challenges are free matches. No money is held or paid out.
  • Bounty matches: a player (the host) may post a bounty, a prize for anyone who beats them, between ₦500 and ₦100,000 (₵5 to ₵2,000). The bounty plus a hosting fee of 20% of the bounty is taken from the host's wallet when the bounty is posted and held by CrestArena. Accepting a bounty is free: the challenger never pays anything. If the challenger wins, they receive the bounty (paid in their own wallet currency at the market rate if it differs). If the host wins, or the match is a draw, the bounty is returned to the host. The host can never gain money from their own bounty. The hosting fee is kept only when the match is played to a result; if the bounty is cancelled, expires unaccepted after 24 hours, or the match is cancelled, the bounty and the fee are refunded in full.
  • Real-money stakes between two players are not offered.
  • Both players must play the agreed game in a player-vs-player mode. Matches against the computer, scenarios, events or campaigns do not count.
  • A result must be uploaded within 3 hours of the challenge being accepted, or the match is cancelled. Unaccepted challenges expire after 24 hours.

2.3 Tournaments

  • There are no entry fees. Joining a CrestArena tournament uses one of the tournament entries your membership plan gives you (section 2.5). An entry is returned if you leave, or the tournament is cancelled, before it starts. Each tournament may limit how many seats go to players on the free plan, and may open early to members (or to Elite members only) before everyone else.
  • Each tournament shows its fixed prizes before anyone joins, in Cedi and in Naira. Prizes are paid by CrestArena, are the same for every player whatever their plan, and do not depend on how many people joined or paid for a membership. Winners are paid in their own wallet currency.
  • Player-hosted cups: any player may host a cup. The host chooses the game, format, number of players (4 to 64) and prize split, and puts up the prize pool (between ₦2,000 and ₦500,000, or ₵20 to ₵5,000) plus a hosting fee of 20% of the pool, taken from their wallet when the cup is created and held by CrestArena. Joining a hosted cup is free, does not use a membership entry, and there is no limit on how many you may join. The prizes shown are paid from the pool to the winners automatically when the cup ends; a winner whose wallet is in the other currency is paid the same value at the market rate. The host may play in their own cup. If the cup does not fill within 7 days, or the host cancels it before it starts, the pool and the fee are refunded to the host in full and players are told. Elite members may host one cup per 30-day plan with the hosting fee waived (the host still funds the pool). A host may have at most 3 cups waiting to fill at a time.
  • Knockout: lose once and you are out; the prizes shown go to the champion (and runner-up if shown). Points (Swiss): everyone plays a set number of rounds against players on similar points; the top places on the table win. League: everyone plays everyone once; the top places win. Win = 3 points, draw = 1; ties are split by goal difference, then goals scored.
  • Fixtures are drawn when the tournament fills. Each round has a deadline. If a match is not played in time, an admin decides the outcome (for example a walkover, a replay or elimination of both players).
  • Knockout matches must have a winner; a draw is decided by the penalty shoot-out shown on the result screen.
  • Prizes are paid only to players who are 18 or older and who pass any identity checks we ask for.

2.4 Results and disputes

  • Either player may upload a screenshot of the final full-time result screen showing both players' names. Our system reads it automatically and checks the names, the game mode and that the screenshot hasn't been used before.
  • The opponent then has 15 minutes to confirm or dispute. If they do nothing, the result becomes final and is settled automatically.
  • Disputed or unclear results are decided by a CrestArena admin, who may ask both players for more evidence. The admin's decision is final, subject to section 9.
  • We may correct a result later if we find fraud or an obvious error, and recover winnings paid as a result.
