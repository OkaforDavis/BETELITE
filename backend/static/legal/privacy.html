3. Why we use it (legal basis)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012, we rely on:

  • Contract: to create your account, run matches and tournaments, verify results, hold stakes, pay winnings and process deposits and withdrawals.
  • Legal obligation: to confirm age, keep financial records, prevent fraud and money laundering, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the platform fair and secure (detecting cheating, reused screenshots and multiple accounts), and to fix and improve the service. We balance these against your rights.
  • Consent: for push notifications and marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time in Profile, without affecting anything done before.

Some providers (including Render, Google, Anthropic and LiveKit) process data outside Nigeria and Ghana, for example in the United States. Where this happens we rely on the safeguards allowed by law, such as contractual data protection commitments from the provider, and we transfer only what is needed.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your personal details, profile photo, game IDs, notifications and push subscriptions straight away.
  • Financial and match records (transactions, deposits, withdrawals, results): kept for [6] years after the transaction to meet tax, accounting and anti-money-laundering laws. After account deletion these records are anonymised and bank account numbers are masked.
  • Match screenshots: 30 days after the match is final, then deleted automatically. What was read from them is kept with the match record.
  • Server logs: up to [30] days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data and take a copy: use Profile → Download my data, or contact us.
  • Correct inaccurate data: edit your profile, or contact us (game IDs lock after your first match to stop cheating; support can correct genuine mistakes).
  • Delete your data: use Profile → Delete account (after withdrawing your balance), subject to the records we must keep by law.
  • Object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent for notifications or marketing at any time.
  • Not be subject to a decision with legal or similar effect based solely on automated processing. Screenshot checks are automated, but your opponent can dispute any result and every dispute is decided by a person.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) or Ghana's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.org.gh).

We respond to requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are managed by Google Firebase. Card details stay with Paystack. Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it. Money movements are recorded in a ledger and checked for consistency. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Age restriction

CrestArena is only for people aged 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth and block anyone under 18. If we learn that a child has an account, we will close it and delete their data.
