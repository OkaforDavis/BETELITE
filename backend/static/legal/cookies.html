Cookie Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, IndexedDB, the app cache) are small pieces of data stored on your device. CrestArena uses only strictly necessary storage: the app cannot work without it. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so we don't ask you to opt in to any.

Third-party services

  • Paystack: when you make a deposit, Paystack's secure checkout opens and may set its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud. These are covered by Paystack's cookie and privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts: our fonts load from Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

Managing storage

You can clear this data in your browser or phone settings (for an installed app, removing it from your Home Screen clears it). If you do, you will be signed out and the app will download again next time.

Changes

If we ever want to use optional cookies (for example, analytics), we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.
