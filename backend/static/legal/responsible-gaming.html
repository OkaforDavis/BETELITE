Responsible Gaming

Effective 1 October 2026

CrestArena is built for competitive fun. Playing for money should never cause harm to you, your finances or the people around you. These are the commitments we make and the tools available to you.

Our commitments

  • 18+ only. Everyone must confirm their date of birth, and we may ask for ID.
  • Limits on stakes: 1v1 stakes are capped, and you can have no more than three open challenges at a time.
  • No credit: you can only play with money already in your wallet.
  • Clear costs: fees and prizes are shown before you commit any money.
  • No pressure: we don't send promotional messages unless you opt in.

Tools you can ask for

Email [support@yourdomain.com] from your account email to set any of these. We act within 24 hours:

  • Deposit limit: a maximum you can deposit per day, week or month.
  • Time-out: a break from paid play for 24 hours to 6 weeks.
  • Self-exclusion: block your account from paid play for 6 months or longer. During self-exclusion we won't send you any promotions, and the exclusion can't be lifted early.

Signs it may be time to take a break

  • Playing to win back money you've lost.
  • Spending more money or time than you planned.
  • Borrowing money, or using money meant for bills, food or school, to play.
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or low when you're not playing.
  • Hiding how much you play from family or friends.

Tips

  • Set a budget before you play and stick to it. Treat stakes as the cost of entertainment, not a way to earn money.
  • Take regular breaks. Don't play when you're upset, tired or under the influence.
  • Keep gaming balanced with work, study, family and rest.

Getting help

If you or someone you know is struggling, help is available and confidential:

  • Gamblers Anonymous: gamblersanonymous.org (international meetings and online support)
  • GamCare: gamcare.org.uk (free online chat available internationally)

Protecting minors

If you share a device with anyone under 18, sign out after playing and keep your password private. Parental-control tools for phones and browsers can block gaming and payment sites.
